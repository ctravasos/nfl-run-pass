How it works

What it shows

Pick an offense and a defense. Every chart puts two bars side by side: how often the offense runs or passes in a situation, and how often its opponents run or pass against the defense in the same situation. Each label reads {{ current }}% ({{ season_range }} avg%), so you can see whether this season matches the longer trend. The five-season average includes {{ current }}.

Which plays count

Regular-season and playoff plays that nflverse classifies as a run or a pass, {{ n_plays }} in all. Sacks and scrambles count as passes. Kneel-downs, spikes, special teams and plays wiped out by a penalty are left out.

Situations

  • Distance: Short is 1–3 yards to go, Medium 4–7, Standard 8–10, Long more than 10.
  • Field position: Own Territory is inside your own 20, Own Half is your 21 to your 49, Opponent Half is midfield to their 21, Red Zone is their 20 and in.
  • Score differential: from the offense's point of view in the offense bars and the defense's point of view in the defense bars. "Up 7–13" on the defense side means the defense's team was leading by 7 to 13.
  • First play of drive: the first run or pass of each drive.
  • First play after timeout: a team's first run or pass after a timeout called during its possession, plus its first run or pass after the end of the 1st and 3rd quarters.
  • Under 2 min in half: 2nd or 4th quarter with 2:00 or less on the clock.
  • Previous drive ended with: for the offense, how its own previous drive in the game ended. For the defense, how the previous drive against it ended. Choosing a subset limits the view to the first play of each drive, because that's when the previous result is freshest.

In the Custom Filter tab, the {{ current }} chart ignores the Year filter, so it always shows this season. Views with fewer than 10 current-season plays are flagged as a small sample.

Data

Play-by-play from nflverse, updated daily during the season; team colors from nflverse as well. The site rebuilds every morning. Source code: github.com/ctravasos/nfl-run-pass.
